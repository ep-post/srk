Teaching

A practitioner–scholar approach connecting more than two decades of field experience with academic analysis of gender, migration and human rights.

Shafiqur Rahman Khan is a practitioner–scholar whose teaching focuses on the intersection of gender, migration, and human rights, drawing on more than two decades of grassroots experience addressing bride trafficking, forced marriage migration, and gender-based exploitation in India.

His teaching is grounded in the belief that meaningful learning emerges from the interaction between lived experience, research, and critical inquiry — connecting field-based knowledge with academic analysis through lectures, seminars, and short courses.

Sample course

Bride Trafficking, Gender Imbalance and Marriage Migration in India — examines the relationship between demographic change, migration, and trafficking within marriage systems, exploring how declining sex ratios and economic inequalities have contributed to cross-regional marriage practices. Students analyse structural drivers, lived experiences, and policy and community responses.
